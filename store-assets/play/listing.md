## Customer app — Balaji Provision Store

**Title (≤30):** Balaji Provision Store

**Short description (≤80):**
Order everyday groceries from Balaji Provision Store. Cash on delivery.

**Full description (≤4000):**
Balaji Provision Store brings your neighbourhood grocery shop to your phone.

Browse the live catalogue, check the price and available stock, add items to
your cart and place a cash-on-delivery order for delivery in the store's service
area. Prices, stock, delivery charges and totals are calculated by the store and
confirmed with your order.

• Live grocery catalogue with prices and stock
• Search and browse by category
• Save delivery addresses, with an optional current-location helper
• Cash-on-delivery checkout
• Order tracking and delivery updates
• Order history for past purchases
• Delete your account and personal data from the app

This version supports cash on delivery only. Online payment is disabled. The app
is intended for customers aged 18 and over in the store's delivery area. It is
not directed to children.

## Staff app — Balaji Staff

**Title (≤30):** Balaji Staff

**Short description (≤80):**
Manage orders, deliveries and stock for Balaji Provision Store.

**Full description (≤4000):**
Balaji Staff is the internal operations app for Balaji Provision Store.

Managers and authorised staff can review incoming orders, update fulfilment and
delivery status, and manage stock. The app hands managers to the store's secure
web dashboard for billing, reports and catalogue editing. The signed-in role
controls which screens are available. This app is not a customer storefront and
is not intended for public use.

• Role-based staff sign-in
• Order and delivery queue
• Delivery status updates
• Stock overview and adjustments
• Handoff to the web dashboard for billing, reports and catalogue editing
• Privacy policy available from the sign-in screen

Access is created by the store owner. Customers should install Balaji Provision
Store instead.
