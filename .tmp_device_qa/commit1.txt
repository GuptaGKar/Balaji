feat(android): orange-glass customer UI from the redesign brief

Applies the Changes/ brief's recommended values across the customer app, on
a branch, with device verification deliberately left to the owner.

Tokens and surfaces (WP1)
- ui/theme/BalajiDesign.kt: one source of truth for colour, radius, rhythm,
  motion timings, the 85% slide threshold and the 48 dp touch floor. The
  existing Color.kt already matched the brief's hexes; only #FFF4EC was new.
- ui/components/BalajiGlass.kt: glass surface, peach information card and the
  single solid-orange purchasing button. No Modifier.blur anywhere: a blur on
  a scrolling product grid costs frames on the mid-range phone this store
  runs on, and the reference cards read as flat warm surfaces.
- ui/theme/Type.kt: semantic scale (Brand/PageTitle/SectionTitle/ProductName/
  Price/Mrp/Total/Quantity/Body/Cta/Metadata/Nav) mapped onto the Material
  roles so existing screens inherit it. NOT DONE: the brief asks for Poppins,
  Inter and Manrope. No licensed binaries exist in this repo or on this
  machine and downloads are not permitted here, so BalajiFont resolves all
  three families to the platform sans-serif. The swap point is three lines in
  one file; the typography is not the reference's typography until that lands.

Motion (WP2)
- AnimatedAddQuantityControl: ADD morphs to [- n +], the digit slides in the
  direction of the change, and the mode/digit/slide rules are pure functions
  with tests. stepQty is honoured so a two-pack line shows "2" and not "1000".
- SlideToConfirm: 85% threshold, spring-back on early release, one commit per
  gesture (the track locks until the caller clears Submitting), haptic once,
  and an accessibility onClick that calls the identical callback - TalkBack
  and Switch Access are not asked to perform a drag.

Pill navigation (WP6)
- NavPill rewritten: surface alpha 0.30 -> 0.94, which is the brief's own
  resolution of BUG-012, plus a solid orange capsule that springs between
  destinations and a reduce-motion path read from the system animator scale.
  Reserve constants are unchanged, so every navPillReserve() call site still
  clears the overlay.

Screens
- Home: orange brand header with the delivery location and the store's real
  status on glass, a capability strip built from flags that exist, and the
  closed-store panel ahead of the banners when the store is shut.
- Product cards: one control row per card (a 48 dp stepper and a price do not
  share a 148 dp card), no duplicated discount text, semantic type roles.
- Cart: "Slide to Checkout" - the gesture navigates, so the label says so.
- Checkout: "Slide to Place Order" only on the step that calls place_order;
  the wizard's intermediate steps stay buttons.
- Product Details: savings stated in money from the two server prices, and the
  sticky CTA carries the line amount it will create.
- Tracking: the in-progress node is orange with a dot instead of a green
  check, and the "shortly" line is gone - nothing knows when a partner is
  assigned.

Not run / not done: no device pass (owner's instruction), no staff-flavour
build yet, and the Categories left-rail was not adopted - the current grid is
the brief's own conditional fallback, and rewriting a working 889-line screen
to match a mockup is not a change to make blind.

Evidence: :app:compileCustomerDebugKotlin clean; :app:testCustomerDebugUnitTest
263 tests, 0 failures (22 new). The new tests assert the arithmetic and the
wording only - no animation, gesture or layout claim is made here, and none of
this has been looked at on a device.
