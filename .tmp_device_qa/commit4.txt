docs: what the redesign pass built, what it did not verify, and one claim I had to correct

Four files, and the point of all four is the same: keep the record of this pass
separable from the record of what it proved.

UI_REDESIGN_REPORT.md is new, and follows the brief's own §18 template so the
gaps land in the slots the brief reserved for them rather than in a footnote.
Sections 4 (screenshots) and 5 (motion clips) are empty because no device pass
was done - the owner asked for the UI and said they would test it - and section
13's scorecard says "not compared" per screen instead of inventing a fidelity
percentage the brief explicitly forbids. Section 9 is "not measured": the two
performance decisions in this pass (no blur anywhere, 0-3 dp elevation inside
lazy grids) were taken on expectation, not on a frame trace, and saying
"smooth" would have been the unsupported claim the brief warns against.

QA_LOG.md gains BUG-026 through BUG-028 and an executed-checks table with the
command next to every result.
- BUG-026 is the one real defect the new pgTAP suite caught before commit: two
  functions that had not shipped yet already disagreed about the horizon - one
  counted store-local days, the other counted instants - so the last permitted
  day would have been offered on Home and refused at checkout. Third instance
  of the BUG-015 / BUG-025 family, first one caught by a test instead of by a
  screen.
- BUG-027 is the fonts. Not a code defect: the brief names Poppins, Inter and
  Manrope, none of them exists here, and downloading is not permitted. Logged
  as an open gap so "typography done" cannot be read into the pass.
- BUG-028 came out of writing the header. White on #FF6B00 is 2.85:1, which
  fails WCAG AA for large text, not only for normal; white on #D95400 is 4.03:1.
  Every orange purchasing control in the app carries a white label, and the
  brief asks for exactly that. The header was rearranged around it, but the
  button fill is one theme value shared by all three apps and is the store's
  brand, so the three ways out are recorded with their measured ratios and left
  for the owner rather than decided in a UI commit.
- BUG-012 moves from OPEN to changed-on-branch: the brief's §10 asks for
  0.90-0.96 in writing, which is the owner revising the 0.30 instruction, so
  GlassAlpha is 0.94. It still needs the light-theme pass on the A34 to close.

docs/PROJECT_STATUS.md gets the branch, and one correction I should have made
long ago. The file claimed suites 001-044 had "still never been run in this
environment - no Docker socket and no local Postgres". A local Supabase stack
(`balaji-store`) is running and is reachable with
`docker exec supabase_db_balaji-store psql`. Whether it was up when that line
was written I cannot say from here; what is certain is that the sentence is now
false, it made the verification story look weaker than it is, and it is the
reason I did not try the local route sooner. It is replaced with what is true
today and how to do it, because the next person will otherwise repeat the same
wrong assumption.
It also records that 20261106000001 is NOT applied to the live project.

docs/DECISIONS.md gets the six places the reference board could not decide -
fonts, blur, four tabs not five, the stepper's own row, closed means closed,
and why Categories kept its grid - plus the §11.1 audit of what the closed
store actually does today and the WP8 line: what is arithmetic shipped, what
would have been invented did not.
