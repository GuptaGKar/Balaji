fix(android): keep the slider orange while it works, and put the wordmark on a fill it reads on

Two defects found while writing the report, both in code committed an hour earlier.

SlideToConfirm went grey mid-submit. The checkout caller disables the control while
`placeOrder` is in flight, because "can the customer act again" is genuinely false at
that moment - and `enabled` was also driving the track fill, the label colour and the
thumb. So the most important gesture in the app turned the disabled colour at exactly
the instant it was working, which reads as "this broke" rather than "this is running".
The fill now follows the state as well as the flag: Submitting and Success keep the
orange, and the `disabled()` accessibility node is withheld for those states too, so
TalkBack says "Working" instead of "Not available".

The Home header had white text on #FF6B00, which is 2.85:1 - below WCAG AA for large
text, not just for normal. The brief's own palette causes it, and the brand colour is
not mine to change, so this fixes only the surface that could be rearranged: the
gradient now puts #D95400 (white = 4.03:1) behind the wordmark and the address line,
with the brighter orange falling away below the opaque glass card, and the avatar
became a near-white disc with a deep-orange initial instead of white-on-translucent.
The remaining white-on-orange labels on the buttons are logged as BUG-028 with three
options and their measured ratios, for the owner to choose between.

AddQuantityControl's doc comment no longer claims a 48 dp hit box for the ADD pill.
The pill is 40 dp tall; whether Compose 1.5.4 expands it to 48 inside a fixed-height
parent is measurable on the handset and was not measured here, so the comment says
which is which instead of rounding the uncertainty away.

Both flavours compile after these edits; the customer unit suite is re-run against
this revision.
