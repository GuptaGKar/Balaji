BALAJI PROVISION STORE — DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

You can erase your customer account and its personal data.

DELETE IN THE APP
1. Sign in to the Balaji Provision Store app.
2. Open Profile.
3. Under Privacy & account, choose Delete my account.
4. Type DELETE and confirm.

The app signs you out and removes your name, phone number, saved addresses and sign-in credentials. Active orders must be completed or cancelled first.

REQUEST BY PHONE
Call 9837803293 from the mobile number registered to your account and ask for account deletion. We will verify control of the number before processing the request.

WHAT IS DELETED AND WHAT IS RETAINED
We delete your profile name, phone, login identifiers, saved addresses, location coordinates and active sign-in sessions. Completed order and bill amounts are retained in an anonymised form for accounting, tax and legal records; item names, quantities and totals remain without your personal details. We do not keep your phone number or address for marketing after deletion.

You can also request deletion by calling the store. We will verify control of the registered number before processing the request.
