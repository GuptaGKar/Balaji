BALAJI PROVISION STORE — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 23 October 2026

WHO WE ARE
Balaji Provision Store is a neighbourhood grocery store in India. This policy explains how the Balaji Provision Store customer and staff apps handle personal information. Contact us on 9837803293.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT
- Account details: name, verified mobile number and account status.
- Delivery details: recipient name, phone, address and landmark.
- Approximate or precise location, only when you choose the location helper while saving an address. You can type an address without granting location access.
- Order details: items, quantities, prices, delivery status, notes and bills.
- Payment method and status for cash-on-delivery orders. We do not receive card numbers.
- Basic security and service logs generated by our hosting provider.
- Staff accounts: login ID, role and work actions (orders, stock, billing) needed to operate the store. The staff app does not collect device location.

We do not use advertising SDKs, sell personal data, or collect contacts, SMS, photos or files in this version of the app.

HOW WE USE IT
- To create and secure your account and verify your mobile number.
- To price, pack, deliver and support your orders.
- To show order history, bills and delivery updates.
- To prevent fraud, keep financial records and meet legal obligations.

WHO CAN SEE IT
Store staff and delivery staff see the details needed to fulfil your order. Our database, authentication and file storage are hosted by Supabase as a service provider. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the store and its customers. We do not share data with advertisers.

RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION
You can delete your account under Profile > Privacy & account > Delete my account. Active orders must be completed or cancelled first. Deletion removes your name, phone number, saved addresses and credentials, and disables sign-in. Completed order and bill amounts are kept in an anonymised form for accounting, tax and legal records. You can also call the store to request deletion.

SECURITY
Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access is restricted by account roles and database security policies. No system is completely secure; contact us immediately if you believe your account has been misused.

CHILDREN
The app is not directed to children under 13. Accounts must be created by a person old enough to enter into a delivery contract.

YOUR CHOICES
You can update your name and saved addresses in the app. For access, correction or deletion requests, use the in-app deletion flow or call the store. We may need to verify control of the registered mobile number before acting on a request.

CHANGES
We may update this policy when the service changes. The latest version will be available in the app and on the store website.
