USING THE SERVICE
The Balaji Provision Store app lets you browse the live catalogue, place a cash-on-delivery order and track delivery within the store's service area. You must provide accurate contact and delivery details and keep your account secure.

ORDERS AND PRICES
Prices, available stock, delivery charges and totals are calculated by the store when you request a quote and place an order. The app does not set prices. An order is confirmed only after the store accepts it. If an item is unavailable, the store may remove it and recalculate the total; you will see the updated order in the app.

PAYMENT
This version supports cash on delivery only. Online payment is disabled. Pay the amount shown on the order or bill at the time of delivery.

CANCELLATIONS AND REJECTION
You can cancel while an order is awaiting store confirmation. After that, call the store. The store may reject an order when delivery is unavailable, stock cannot be fulfilled, or the details cannot be verified.
